1. Information We Process

Platform and version scope

The iPhone App Store release, version 1.0.0, has advertising and in-app purchases disabled. It does not initialize the advertising SDK or request ads, and it does not offer Remove Ads purchases or purchase restoration. The Google Mobile Ads and Google Play billing descriptions below apply to Android Google Play releases with those features enabled. The descriptions of question submissions, reports, server access logs, on-device storage, and result-image saving also apply to the iOS release.

Question submissions

  • An app-generated device ID
  • The question, choices A and B, and optional category you submit
  • Submission time and review status

Question reports

  • An app-generated device ID
  • The reported question ID, report reason, and optional details you enter
  • Report time

Information processed automatically

  • IP address, country inferred from the IP address, and operating system, device, or browser information included in a user agent
  • Request time, App version, and network request or response information
  • For advertising: approximate location inferred from IP address, App and ad interactions, diagnostics, Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and similar device or account identifiers

The server provider may process access-log information automatically for security and troubleshooting. Separately, the advertising SDK in Android releases with ads enabled may process advertising information for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. The iOS 1.0.0 release does not request advertising. The App does not send your contacts, precise location, microphone recordings, or existing photos to our server.

Google Play in-app purchases

When you buy Remove Ads or restore a purchase, Google Play processes your billing account and payment method. The App receives the product ID, purchase state, and transaction identifier through Google Play Billing to apply the ad-free entitlement. The App does not store payment-method details or purchase tokens in local storage or on the Developer's server.

Information stored only on your device

  • The app-generated device ID and settings such as dark mode and sound effects
  • Favorites, answers, play statistics, daily-question history, and streak history
  • Personality-quiz results and local copies of submissions and reports

This local information remains on your device unless you use a feature that sends the specific information described above. If operating-system backup is enabled, local App information may be included in your personal device backup.

Saving and sharing result images

The App requests photo-library access only when you choose to save a result image. It saves only an image created by the App and does not read or upload your existing photos. If you choose to share, the operating system sends the result image or text to the app or person you select.

2. How We Use Information

  • Review submitted questions for duplicates, safety, and possible inclusion in the App
  • Receive and review reports and enforce our content standards
  • Reduce repeated or abusive submissions and reports
  • Diagnose errors, prevent security incidents, and operate the service reliably
  • Provide and measure advertising and prevent ad fraud
  • Provide the local settings, gameplay, result-saving, and sharing features you request

3. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit a question, file a report, save a result image, or share a result. If you do not use an optional feature, only that feature is unavailable.

You may reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings and limit personalized advertising through the privacy controls available on your device. Where applicable law requires consent before advertising information is processed, the App will request that consent.

Advertising

The Android Google Play build may request banner ads on the Personality Quiz screen, interstitial ads after every 5 newly completed random or category questions only when at least 5 minutes have passed since the previous interstitial, and a rewarded ad only when the user explicitly chooses it to retake the personality quiz. Ads are not requested while the Remove Ads entitlement is active. Requests use the non-personalized-ads option, but Google and its advertising partners may still process the advertising information described in Section 1 to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. The Developer does not directly sell your personal information. Advertising processing may qualify as a sale or sharing under some state privacy laws, and applicable device or consent controls will be provided where required.

6. International Processing

The Developer operates from South Korea. Our providers and their support or security teams may process information in South Korea, Singapore, the United States, and other countries where they operate. Privacy laws in those countries may differ from those where you live. We use encrypted network connections and rely on provider contractual and security safeguards for these transfers.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information associated with your submissions or reports, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have the right to appeal a denied request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

Use Reset Play Data in Settings to delete local favorites, answers, statistics, daily-question history, and personality results. Uninstall the App or clear its data in operating-system settings to remove all local information, including settings and the app-generated device ID.

8. Security

  • HTTPS encryption for information in transit
  • Supabase Row Level Security that blocks anonymous reading, editing, or deleting of server records
  • Input-length limits, allowed report-reason validation, and control-character removal
  • Rate limits intended to reduce repeated submissions and reports
  • Limited administrative access and error logs restricted to development environments

No security measure is perfect, but we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information processed by the App.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it. Do not enter your own or another person's name, contact information, address, financial account information, or other identifying information in a question or report.

10. Third-Party Services and Links

The operating-system share sheet and links in this policy may take you to services we do not control. Information you choose to send to another app or website is governed by that service's privacy practices.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App, our providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the Last updated date and provide additional notice in the App or on the published policy page when a change materially affects your rights or how we use information.
